Executive Summary

Snapshot of current SEO health

I reviewed Search Console and the site started losing visitors from October 9, 2025. The 16-month performance view (135K clicks / 6.54M impressions) shows a clear peak on Thursday, Oct 9 — 814 clicks / 22,305 impressions — followed by a sustained decline.

On October 7–8, 2025 Google underwent a massive change, though it was not announced as a core update. As reported by Barry Schwartz, Executive Editor of Search Engine Roundtable, Google started AI Mode expansion during this window.

Around October 15–17, a second wave focused on EEAT and thin / over-optimized AI text rolled out.

From your Search Console pattern I can confirm two phases: it was first hit during the base AI expansion where different direct keywords lost visibility, then likely hit again due to AI-text content quality.

Technical Issues

Items requiring immediate attention

EEAT issue

Server Connectivity — High Fail Rate

In recent time the site had huge server issues. Google reports Host had problems last week and was not always able to reach content. This cannot be fixed by SEO — you need a stable server / hosting.

Wrong Sitemap

Sitemap contains wrong / outdated URLs — needs rebuild and resubmit in Search Console.

Robots.txt Issues

Robots.txt directives misconfigured — needs review to avoid blocking important pages.

Many 404 Pages

Multiple not-found URLs detected — redirect to relevant pages or restore, and update internal links.

Top Search Queries

Branded vs non-branded overview

Branded strength: Based on the first 10 queries, more than 50% of traffic comes from branded search — which is good for the site.

Strong brand demand provides a stable base. Next focus should be expanding non-branded queries to grow beyond brand traffic.

Priority Recommendations

Ordered by impact vs effort

  • Fix technical SEO first — stabilize server/hosting, correct sitemap, robots.txt, 404s and structured data.
  • Then content — revise according to consultation, fix thin / over-optimized AI text and improve EEAT.
  • Allow 3–6 months — everything may need 3–6 months to observe cleanly in Search Console.
  • Team needed — you need a good web developer who can optimize for PageSpeed Insights, and a good content writer who can change according to consultation.
